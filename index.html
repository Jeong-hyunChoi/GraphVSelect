Abstract

Referring Expression Comprehension localizes an image region described by natural language. Existing zero-shot approaches typically rely on proposal matching or independent box-wise verification, providing limited explicit comparison among candidates. Recent scene graph-based methods introduce structured relational evidence, but construct the graph in a single pass, making subsequent reasoning sensitive to inaccurate objects. We present GraphVSelect, a training-free framework for reliable zero-shot REC through verified graph-guided selection. Our method hierarchically verifies candidates before constructing a query-conditioned scene graph. It then jointly ranks all candidates under the verified relational context and selectively re-examines ambiguous predictions using the top-1/top-2 likelihood margin. Across RefCOCO/+/g and the challenging Ref-L4 with multiple vision-language models, GraphVSelect improves over the strongest zero-shot baseline by a relative 15.0% and 16.6%, respectively, with the largest gains on long, compositionally complex expressions.

Overview

GraphVSelect has two stages, both built on frozen off-the-shelf models. Hierarchically Verified Scene Graph Construction asks a VLM to verify every detector proposal and every generated caption before the graph is assembled: spurious objects are removed, missing referents are re-detected, and captions are rewritten until they distinguish an object from its same-category siblings. Confidence-Aware Structured Selection then scores all candidates in a single list-wise query over the verified graph, reading constrained first-token log-likelihoods. The top-1/top-2 margin decides whether the answer is accepted or re-asked between the two leading candidates in both presentation orders, so extra reasoning is spent only where the decision is ambiguous.

Results

GraphVSelect achieves the best accuracy on every RefCOCO/+/g split and on Ref-L4, using Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct without any task-specific training. The gains are largest on long, compositional expressions and persist under strict IoU thresholds.
